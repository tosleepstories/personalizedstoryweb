You print

We send print-ready files. You print and post them like your other photo books.

  • Interior.pdf: 8.5″ × 8.5″ trim plus 0.125″ bleed (8.75″ page), 24 pages or more
  • Full cover wrap.pdf: back, spine and front with the same bleed
  • Separate front, spine and back files if your RIP prefers them
  • Colour; glossy or matte laminated hardcover works well. Don’t scale to A4.
  • If our PDF is ever the wrong spec, we regenerate it at our cost

Photos and children’s data

Careful with children’s photos

  • Photos are used only to make that one book, and are never used to train image or language models.
  • Source photos are deleted after 7 days, or sooner on request. Artwork and PDFs are kept up to 30 days for reprints, then deleted.
  • We don’t need your customers’ emails, addresses or payment details. Those stay with you.
  • We don’t profile children, build audiences from faces, or put photos in ads. For EU/UK orders we act as processor; you’re the controller.

Questions partners ask

FAQ

What does it cost?

The pilot is free: for 90 days in one country, you keep 100% of retail on your first 50 paid orders and we cover the AI costs. After that it’s €8 per book for generation, or 20% of your retail price, whichever you choose. Printing, shipping and pricing to your customer stay with you.

What if a cartoon doesn’t look like the child?

The customer approves every likeness before paying. If a face isn’t right, we redraw it, or the order is cancelled before print. During the pilot we pay for the redraw. You handle the message to your customer.

What files do we get, and will they fit our press?

An interior PDF (8.5″ square trim, 0.125″ bleed, 24+ pages) and a full cover wrap (back, spine, front). Separate front, spine and back files are available too. If a PDF is ever the wrong spec, we regenerate it at our cost.

Do we need developers to start?

Not for a trial: send a real order through the form above. For a live product page, your team connects to our job API: photos and names in, cartoon previews out, print PDFs back after approval.

Who talks to the customer?

You do. You sell, charge and support the customer. We only receive photos, first names and story choices, and we never email your customers.

Which stories, ages and languages are available?

Seven settings (forest walk, backyard garden, seaside, stars on the hill, snowy day, birthday picnic, hidden meadow), read-aloud ages 3–5, 6–9 and 10–12, up to six characters, in English, Portuguese, Spanish, French, German or Italian.
